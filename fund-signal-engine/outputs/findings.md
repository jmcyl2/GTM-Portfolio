# Findings

_Generated by `build.py` from public SEC data. Summary stats only, no personal data._

Terms used below: an *adviser* is the firm that manages one or more funds. *PE/RE* means private equity and real estate. A fund that reports no outside fund administrator does its own books, and is called *self-administered*.

## Data

- Fund records (Form ADV, Schedule D 7.B.(1)): **2,014,331**, from filings dated 2011-11-08 to 2024-12-26. The administrator question is answered on **100%** of them.
- Which advisers are active today, and their current size, comes from the SEC's adviser list: `ia100226-exempt.zip, ia100226.zip`.

## Backtest: did the v1 score predict which funds hired an administrator?

Funds tested: **19,160** PE/RE funds that did their own books in some year from 2014 to 2021 (each fund counted once, in its first such year), then checked again 3 years later. **9.8%** had hired an outside administrator by then.

The score was written down in `sql/03_backtest.sql` before any results were looked at: 1 point per signal.

| Signal | Funds with signal | Hired admin (with signal) | Hired admin (without) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Audited | 15,973 | 10.6% | 5.8% | 1.83x |
| Adviser $20M-$500M | 5,811 | 8.4% | 10.4% | 0.81x |
| Adviser 1-5 funds | 3,590 | 11.2% | 9.5% | 1.18x |
| Fund assets up 25%+ in a year | 1,373 | 7.3% | 10.0% | 0.73x |

| Score | Funds | Hired admin within 3 yrs |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 912 | 6.8% |
| 1 | 11,907 | 10.0% |
| 2 | 4,325 | 9.3% |
| 3 | 1,874 | 12.0% |
| 4 | 142 | 4.2% |

**Score 3–4 vs 0–1: 11.4% vs 9.7% hired an administrator (1.2x).** Barely better than random.

Caveat: funds that stopped filing within 3 years (closed or merged) are left out, so these rates only cover funds that were still around.

The v1 score is left as it was first written. A score learned from the data and tested on later years is in [score_v2.md](score_v2.md). A test against 2025–26 filings is in [refresh.md](refresh.md).

## Form D: only a sign that something happened

- 24,895 Form D filings from PE, real estate and other funds in the quarters loaded. Only 9% match a Form ADV fund by exact name, so Form D is used as a sign that something new happened at a firm, not to build the target list.
